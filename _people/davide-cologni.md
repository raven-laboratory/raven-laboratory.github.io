Davide earned his master’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Milan in April 2025, with a thesis on graph compression. He then joined the RAVEN research group at Ca' Foscari University of Venice as a Ph.D. student, under the supervision of Professor Giulio Ermanno Pibiri.
His primary research interests are in the field of compressed data structures.
